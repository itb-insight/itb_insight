# How the QR Features Work
Each account is given a unique personal QR code.

## Attendance
If a user steps inside of the premises of Institut Teknologi Bandung Ganesha Campus,
they are instantly marked as "attending". However, to confirm that this person
entered the premises of ITB Insight through an assigned gate, their personal QR code
are scanned by a Gate Staff. This step is NOT necessary for taking note of attendance
(and exit, which is important because we need to track the live visitor count), but
serves as a TUTORIAL to new visitors of how the personal profile QR work through
demonstration of attendance scanning!

## Points Allocation (Booth)
While visiting a booth, the visitors are marked to be within the perimeter by the
geo-fencing service. This may not be updated live if the local internet bandwith
is weak.

During a booth visit, BOOTH STAFF may give points to the visitors by scanning the
visitor's unique profile QR through their booth admin view. There is a "standard value"
for the given points, and a bonus value which can be given freely (up to a certain max)
to represent active participation or winning certain games / activities in the booth.
