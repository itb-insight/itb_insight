## Authority

1. [PRD(v1.0).md](PRD(v1.0).md) is the final product-intent authority: requirements, MoSCoW priorities, constraints, won't-haves, and D-01–D-11.

3. Scope, backlog, and sprints translate the final PRD into phases; they do not reduce a PRD Must requirement to optional work.
